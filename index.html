About

One oven, a short menu.

NOIR opened in 2019 with a single wood-fired oven and a menu that fit on one page. The oven is still the centre of the kitchen. Anything that can take smoke and flame goes on the fire, and the rest we leave alone.

The menu changes when the produce does. We cook to order, so some plates take a little longer. We would rather you wait than eat something that sat under a lamp.

The room

Sit by the hearth

The kitchen is open along one wall, so dinner comes with the sound of the pass: knives, flame, a plate being called out. If you want to watch, ask for a seat at the hearth.

  • The hearth

    Oak and mango wood, burned down to embers before anything goes on the grill.

  • The cellar

    180 labels picked to go with the food, and about a dozen by the glass.

  • The private room

    Twelve seats behind a door of their own, with a menu planned around your group.
